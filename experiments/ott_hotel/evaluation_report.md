## 2. Dataset Partition & Pipeline Specifications

- **Total Corpus Size:** 1,600 reviews (20 Chicago luxury hotels)
- **Train Set:** 1,280 reviews (80.0%), exactly 640 truthful / 640 deceptive
- **Held-Out Test Set:** 320 reviews (20.0%), exactly 160 truthful / 160 deceptive
- **Text-Hash Overlap:** **0 reviews** (Guaranteed zero data leakage via `StratifiedGroupKFold`)
- **Text-Clustering:** All duplicate review pairs strictly kept in identical partitions.

### Pipeline Architecture
1. **Text Vectorizer:** `TfidfVectorizer`
   - `ngram_range`: (1, 2) [unigrams + bigrams]
   - `min_df`: 2
   - `sublinear_tf`: True (applies sublinear scaling $1 + \log(\text{tf})$)
   - `strip_accents`: 'unicode'
   - `lowercase`: True
   - **Fitted Vocabulary Size:** 23,047 features
2. **Classifier:** `LogisticRegression`
   - `C`: 1.0 (L2 regularisation)
   - `max_iter`: 1000
   - `random_state`: 42
   - `solver`: 'lbfgs'

## 6. Critical Limitations of Hotel-Only Training

> [!WARNING]
> **Strict Domain Boundary Notice:**  
> This model was trained exclusively on the Ott et al. (2011, 2013) Chicago Hotel Deception Corpus. It is **NOT** validated for e-commerce products, restaurant reviews, app store feedback, or general service reviews.

Key limitations include:
1. **Domain-Specific Vocabulary:** Hotel terminology (*e.g., 'front desk', 'bellman', 'concierge', 'valet', 'room service', 'suite'*) dominates the n-gram feature weights. When evaluated against product reviews (*e.g., battery life, fit, shipping speed*) or mobile app feedback (*e.g., crash, update, UI bug*), these features will be absent or uninformative.
2. **Artificial Crowd-Worker Generation:** Deceptive reviews in the Ott dataset were generated by Amazon Mechanical Turk workers instructed to imagine staying at a hotel and write a fake review. While this yields clean ground-truth deception, it reflects a specific human writing style (often higher imaginative elaboration, emotional exaggeration, fewer spatial details) and may differ from real-world automated spam bots, incentivized commercial syndicates, or LLM-generated reviews.
3. **Geographic & Category Concentration:** All reviews are from 20 luxury hotels in Chicago. Price range and regional idioms are therefore restricted.
