## 2. In-Depth Candidate Dataset Investigations

### Candidate 1: Ott Deceptive Opinion Spam Corpus (Existing Benchmark)
* **Domain:** Hotels & Hospitality
* **Academic Reference:** Myle Ott, Yejin Choi, Claire Cardie, and Jeffrey T. Hancock. (ACL 2011 / NAACL 2013).
* **Source & Download Link:** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rajsiddarth/Text_Analytics/master/deceptive-opinion.csv`
* **Download Status:** **Successfully Acquired & Verified** (`data/deceptive-opinion.csv`, 1,600 rows).
* **Record Count & Schema:** 1,600 reviews; Columns: `deceptive`, `hotel`, `polarity`, `source`, `text`.
* **Label Distribution:** Exactly 800 `truthful` (50.0%) and 800 `deceptive` (50.0%); balanced across 800 positive and 800 negative reviews across 20 Chicago hotels.
* **Label Assignment Methodology:** Controlled experimental design. Truthful reviews were crawled from TripAdvisor, Expedia, Hotels.com, Orbitz, Priceline, and Yelp. Deceptive reviews were generated by crowdworkers on Amazon Mechanical Turk tasked with producing believable deceptive reviews for hotels they never visited.
* **Label Nature:** **Crowdsourced Controlled Deception** (High-quality experimental ground truth).
* **Data Health:** 0 missing values; 4 duplicate pairs (8 rows total in truthful negative from TripAdvisor crawl, isolated during training via stratified group k-fold).
* **Licensing:** Academic Research Use.
* **Quality Tier:** **Tier A (Suitable for supervised training)**.

* **Domain:** Products & E-Commerce (10 distinct product categories)
* **Academic Reference:** Joni Salminen, Chandrashekhar Kandpal, Ahmed Mohamed Kamel, Soon-gyo Jung, Bernard J. Jansen. (2022). *"Creating and detecting fake reviews of online products."* Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services.

* **Record Count & Schema:** **40,432 reviews**; Columns: `['category', 'rating', 'label', 'text_']`.
* **Label Distribution:** Exactly balanced:
  * `OR` (**Original / Human-Written Genuine Review**): 20,216 (50.0%)
  * `CG` (**Computer-Generated / Fake Review**): 20,216 (50.0%)
* **Category Breakdown (10 Amazon Categories):**
  * Kindle Store: 4,730
  * Books: 4,370
  * Pet Supplies: 4,254
  * Home and Kitchen: 4,056
  * Electronics: 3,988

  * Movies and TV: 3,588
* **Label Assignment Methodology:** Original reviews (`OR`) were sampled from genuine Amazon product reviews. Fake reviews (`CG`) were generated using a fine-tuned GPT-2 text generation model conditioned on product metadata, star rating, and category prompts.

* **Data Health:** 0 missing values; exactly 20 exact duplicate review texts across 40,432 rows. Average word count: 73.6 words for `OR`, 61.3 words for `CG`.
* **Licensing:** Open access under Creative Commons / Academic Research.
* **Quality Tier:** **Tier B (Usable with important label limitations)**.  
  *Justification:* While it provides clean multi-category e-commerce product reviews, the fake reviews are machine-generated rather than crowdsourced or commercial review-farm fraud. It is highly relevant for detecting modern AI-generated product spam, but should be documented as synthetic deception.

### Candidate 3: UCSD Amazon Review Data (2018 Collection)
* **Domain:** Products & E-Commerce (29 categories)
* **Academic Reference:** Jianmo Ni, Jiacheng Li, Julian McAuley. (EMNLP 2019). *"Justifying recommendations using distantly-labeled reviews and fine-grained aspects."*
* **Source & Download Link:** `https://jmcauley.ucsd.edu/data/amazon/`
* **Download Status:** **Accessible Online, But Not Acquired for Deception Ground Truth**.
* **Record Count & Schema:** 233.5 million reviews; Columns: `overall`, `verified`, `reviewTime`, `reviewerID`, `asin`, `style`, `reviewerName`, `reviewText`, `summary`, `unixReviewTime`, `vote`, `image`.
* **Label Assignment Methodology:** **None.** The dataset contains no ground truth fake or deceptive labels.
* **Proxy Evaluated:** The `verified` boolean flag (Verified Purchase).
* **Critical Methodological Finding:**
  > [!WARNING]  
  > **Why Verified Purchase Cannot Serve as Ground Truth:**  
  > In empirical studies, treating `verified == False` as "fake" introduces severe false positives (legitimate customers who received the item as a gift, purchased from a third-party retailer, or reviewed promotional manufacturer samples). Conversely, modern review syndicates actively instruct paid reviewers to purchase products and reimburse them via PayPal to artificially gain the "Verified Purchase" badge. Using `verified` as a fake/real label is scientifically invalid.
* **Licensing:** Academic non-commercial research.
* **Quality Tier:** **Tier C (Not suitable for supervised fake/genuine classification)**.

### Candidate 4: DeRev 2018 Dataset (DEception in REViews)
* **Domain:** Products & E-Commerce (Amazon Books)
* **Academic Reference:** Tommaso Fornaciari and Massimo Poesio. (EACL 2014; Language Resources and Evaluation, 2020).
* **Source & Download Link:** `https://t-fornaciari.netlify.app/` (Direct academic contact required).
* **Download Status:** **Gated / Restricted Access**. No public, unauthenticated direct download endpoint exists.
* **Record Count & Schema:** ~8,311 items; Columns: `text`, `gold_label`, `reviewer_id`, `item_id`, `clues`, `timestamp`.
* **Label Distribution:** Binary gold standard (`0` = Deceptive, `1` = Genuine).
* **Label Assignment Methodology:** Confessed real-world deception. The authors identified reviews authored by individuals who publicly confessed (or were legally exposed in investigative reports) to writing fake Amazon book reviews for financial gain, contrasted against confirmed authentic book reviews. Extended in 2018 (*Crowd-DeRev*) with Mechanical Turk crowdsourced reviews.
* **Label Nature:** **Confirmed In-The-Wild Fraud Ground Truth** (Gold standard in NLP deception literature).
* **Licensing & Access Restriction:** Gated by authors due to copyright constraints on scraped Amazon text and privacy protections for exposed individuals. Access requires signing an academic sharing agreement directly with Dr. Fornaciari.
* **Quality Tier:** **Tier A (Suitable for supervised training)**, but currently unavailable via public direct download.

### Candidate 5: YelpChi Benchmark Dataset
* **Domain:** Restaurants & Hospitality (Chicago)
* **Academic Reference:** Shebuti Rayana and Leman Akoglu. (ACM SIGKDD 2015). *"Collective Opinion Spam Detection: Bridging Review Networks and Metadata."*
* **Source & Download Link:** `https://github.com/YingtongDou/CARE-GNN/raw/master/data/YelpChi.zip`
* **Download Status:** **Downloaded & Inspected** ([`candidate_datasets/YelpChi_CARE_GNN.zip`](file:///c:/Users/himan/Downloads/Fake_Review_Detection_System/Fake_Review_Detection_System/candidate_datasets/YelpChi_CARE_GNN.zip), 18.0 MB).
* **Record Count & Schema (Inside `YelpChi.mat`):** 45,954 records; Attributes: `homo`, `net_rur`, `net_rtr`, `net_rsr`, `features` (45,954 × 32 sparse matrix), `label` (45,954 array: 39,277 legitimate / 6,677 spam).
* **Critical Technical Finding for Text NLP:**
  > [!IMPORTANT]  
  > **Text Stripping in Public GNN Benchmarks:**  
  > Inspection of `YelpChi.mat` reveals that public graph benchmarks distribute **32-dimensional pre-computed numerical features and graph adjacency matrices, but completely omit raw review text (`reviewContent`)**. This was done because Yelp's Terms of Service strictly prohibit the open redistribution of scraped user review text. As a consequence, `YelpChi.mat` cannot be fed into a TF-IDF text pipeline without obtaining the separate raw text archive directly from the authors.
* **Label Nature:** **Platform-Filtered Algorithmic Proxy**.
* **Quality Tier:** **Tier C for Text-Based NLP** (Tier B if raw text is acquired via academic request).

## 5. Schema Compatibility & Harmonization Strategy

To safely merge multi-domain data in future training phases without corrupting labels or conflating ground-truth methodologies, the following **Standardized Multi-Domain Schema** is established:

```text
Standardized Column Schema:
├── review_text        (Normalized clean text content)
├── label              (Binary target: 'genuine' vs 'deceptive')
├── domain             (Category: 'hotel', 'product', 'restaurant', 'service')
├── dataset_source     (Provenance: 'ott_chicago', 'amazon_salminen_40k', 'yelp_restaurant')
├── label_nature       (Distinction: 'crowdsourced_experimental', 'synthetic_ai', 'platform_filtered')
└── original_label     (Raw source value: 'truthful'/'deceptive', 'OR'/'CG', 'Y'/'N')
```

### Safety Principles:
1. **No Conflation:** The training and evaluation pipeline must never treat a platform-filtered review (Yelp) as mathematically identical to a crowdsourced deceptive review (Ott) without an explicit `label_nature` feature or stratified evaluation.
2. **Domain-Wise Stratification:** Multi-domain models must be evaluated with **domain-wise holdouts** (e.g. Test on Products, Test on Hotels, Test on Restaurants) to identify cross-domain generalization vs domain-specific overfitting.
